For the quantity distinction, NOAA's formulation implies quadrature addition of heights only when combining nonoverlapping spectral energy partitions under compatible definitions. It does **not** mean alternative model forecasts should be combined in quadrature. Changing every linear forecast blend to an energy sum would confuse prediction averaging with physical superposition. [NOAA wave calculations](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/faq/wavecalc.shtml).

SWAN's scientific model evolves a spectrum in frequency and direction using an action-balance equation, with propagation and source/sink terms. The app's scalar transforms and visual crests should not be presented as an equivalent spectral solver without demonstrating that architecture and validating it. [SWAN action balance](https://swanmodel.sourceforge.io/online_doc/swantech/node12.html).

## Corrections to inherited scientific claims

**Breaking ceiling:** Carini et al. report wave-by-wave observations at Duck, with separate spilling/plunging ranges. They do not establish the highest breaker index ever possible across all field sites, nor that every steep reef is physically bounded by0.81. The registry and inline comment made those overbroad claims; they are corrected in this patch. Values and flags remain unchanged. “Field beats laboratory beats model” was also replaced with a requirement to match regime and measured quantity. An individual-wave onset ratio and a significant-height transform need a demonstrated transfer argument. [Carini et al.,2021](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2020JC016935).

**Shelf floor:** Ardhuin et al.'s93% attenuation observation concerns particular continental-shelf events and conditions. It is not a globally validated physical bound. The registry already discloses that0.316 corresponds to a90% energy reduction under a square-root conversion and does not equal sqrt(0.07). The study also discusses energy flux; depth/group velocity and the precise compared quantity matter before deriving a height ratio. Do not silently change0.316 to0.265 and call that a universal scientific correction. [Ardhuin et al.,2003](https://journals.ametsoc.org/abstract/journals/phoc/33/9/1520-0485_2003_033_1921_statcs_2.0.co_2.xml).

**Surf-height convention:** H1/10 has a documented role in the Oahu surf climatology and related forecasting practice. That is not evidence that every surf observation worldwide uses that statistic, or that a Rayleigh conversion is exact for depth-limited breaking waves. A display convention must match the observation used to calibrate/validate it. The app's own convention module already records saturation and compensating-error concerns; neither a blanket1.27 multiplier nor removal of it is justified by this review. [University of Hawaii Oahu surf climatology](https://uhslc.soest.hawaii.edu/outreach/climo/oahu_surf_climatlogy.html).

**Ensemble confidence:** member agreement is not guaranteed forecast accuracy. The spread-to-error relationship needs validation; even agreement of averaged spread and error is insufficient by itself for calibration. Keep uncertainty separate from the subjective surf score, and retain `calibrated:false` until outcome evidence warrants changing it. [ECMWF ensemble mean/spread](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/FUG/pages/673551056/Section%2B8.1.2%2BENS%2BMean%2Band%2Bspread), [ECMWF reliability diagnostics](https://charts.ecmwf.int/products/plwww_3m_ens_tigge_spreldiag).

**Forecast comparison:** match quantity, place, valid time and forecast lead, account for observation quality and dependence, and distinguish observation uncertainty from forecast error. A more finely drawn grid or agreement with another model is not an independent observation. [ECMWF verification against observations](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/newsletter/149/meteorology/use-forecast-departures-verification-against-observations).

## Concrete repair and tests from this pass

An independent probe of `forecast_spread.relative_spread` found that infinity could return0/inf/NaN, which the confidence classifier converted into high/low/moderate. This is a reproduced helper-boundary defect; no evidence established that production currently supplies those values. Finite operands could also overflow their ratio.

The bounded repair rejects nonfinite operands and nonfinite ratios as unavailable, preserving the existing null contract and normal finite behavior. Three new cases failed before the first correction; a fourth finite-overflow case failed before the ratio guard. After correction, the complete selected set passes142 tests. Independent dispersion/scaling controls still pass and invalid confidence inputs now return unavailable. No score weights, physical constants, forecast ceilings, renderer gates or visual budgets change.

## Focused path toward a defensible high-quality release

1. Finish exact-head verification of the small validation repair. Preserve the history and source-backed context in canonical memory.
2. Continue the **same immediate visual mission**: fixed resident/incoming products, fixed camera/model/layer/time, deliberately controlled replacement arrival. Observe regional opacity and coarse wash separately; preserve rating-mode snap and coverage protections. A new live run alone is insufficient if the failed condition is absent.
3. Carry source/cycle/quantity identity into remaining skill-ledger boundaries. Preserve unknown historical identity as unknown. This enables causal stratification before calibration.
4. Evaluate held-out skill by lead, geography, sea-state regime and observed quantity against persistence and suitable public references, with uncertainty and missingness reported. Revisit coefficients only when a specified failure mechanism and independent outcomes support it.
5. Close broader domain gates separately: native/estimated disclosure, wave partitions, nearshore observations, wind/current/datum conventions, uncertainty calibration and extremes. Existing canonical task/objective IDs remain authoritative; this report is context, not a competing backlog.

The Jacobian lens is useful for one-factor sensitivity and constrained parameters, but sensitivity is not truth. For the60/40 pair, perturb one weight and its complement together to preserve normalization. For branch thresholds, report one-sided interventions instead of pretending a smooth derivative exists. For causal forecast quality, sensitivity must be followed by independent observations. User intuition and AI suggestions can identify experiments; they cannot determine the experimental outcome.
