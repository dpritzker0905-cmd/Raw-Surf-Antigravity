## Europe insurance check (Copernicus Marine In Situ NRT, 2026-11-23): NOT verified, Europe stays OUT of this test
Bounded check on 11 daily files of 2026-11-20 from the public bucket (product `INSITU_GLO_PHYBGCWAV_DISCRETE_MYNRT_013_030`, file attributes and cadence only; no forecast, skill or model value; no file kept in the repo). Platforms: Met Office buoys K1/K2, Marine Institute M2, Irish Lights Finnis, Bilbao, Belgian Blankenberge/Ostend, UK coastal Folkestone/Looe Bay/Chapel Point, Mesurho (Gulf of Lion).
- (a) **Gust averaging window: not documented.** `GSPD` carries only `standard_name wind_speed_of_gust` and units; no `cell_methods`, comment or window in any of the 11 files, and the cadence differs by provider (hourly for the Met Office, Marine Institute and Irish Lights buoys; 10 min for the Belgian and UK coastal stations; 4 min on Mesurho). It cannot be shown to be the same quantity as NDBC GST, and the G1 truth rule (report nearest each hour, max over 6 h) would give different maxima for hourly and 10-minute series.
- (b) **Anemometer heights: absent for the core set.** `DEPH` is a sensor depth (negative above sea level). Present: Belgian -10 m, Looe -11.3, Chapel Point -9.4, Marine Institute M2 -3.2, Finnis -4, Bilbao -22; Folkestone -79.8 (an 80 m onshore structure, outside 1-50 m). The Met Office buoys carry 0 and 1 m (no height at all). Of the 38 EUROPE_ATLANTIC platforms with gust on >= 70% of the last 31 daily files only 14 are buoy-like (WMO-style codes); 10 of those are Met Office buoys, so the 10-station floor cannot be met with documented heights.
- (c) Rough marine exposure: 5 of 11 are `land/onshore structure` (Belgian, UK coastal); under the registered rule a non-buoy needs a paired catalogue spot within 25 km and an onshore water sector, so they are not safely counted.
- (d) Coverage looks good (24 distinct hours on 24 of 24 days sampled for 10 of 11; 38 platforms with gust in >= 70% of daily files), and the 'latest' dataset is a rolling ~31-day window (a snapshot collector would be needed).
Verdict: (a) and (b) are not verified, so per the instruction Europe stays out of this test: no R2 amendment, no reader, no collector. Europe remains "insufficient" as registered. A later registration could add it after the Met Office / Marine Institute / Irish Lights gust definitions and anemometer heights are documented from the providers (outside the files).
