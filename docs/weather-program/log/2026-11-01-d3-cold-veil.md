## Update: the coastline rule (the lead's request: the owner's pick assumes the preview never paints at a coast)

  polygons: the Natural Earth land MapPage fetches (`getSharedLandGeoJSON`, ne_50m with ne_110m fallback) and the engine masks with
  (`engine._landGeoJSON`). New `marineCoastTest.js`: a ring vertex in the box or a ring edge crossing it (ring bounding boxes indexed
  once per GeoJSON). No land data yet -> suppressed (`no_land_data`, fail closed). Decided once per cold commit (at the stamp, cached
  on the veil by the grid and the view rounded to whole degrees; a pan during the veil re-decides), never per frame. Rules (a)
  pilot boxes and (b) land/no-data nodes are unchanged.
