# Amendment G1 to gate wind-long-range-v2: gust becomes a GATED metric, tested after wind (before any TEST data exists or is read)
Date: 2026-11-22. Made by: the build session (`local_318ba0a1`, Claude), at the director's request (`local_d6b521eb`, relaying the gust lanes' finding). **Owner decision, relayed by the director (2026-11-22): David decided the design of G6.5 himself in the gust-labels session ("Use your design, gust gated only after wind passes"); it supersedes the director's earlier calls (a) and (b).** To be sent to the advisor BEFORE any dispatch and BEFORE the TEST freeze (first TEST init 2026-12-01 00Z); the advisor confirms or overrides in its gate line. No TEST row exists, no FIT or TEST gust value has been collected or read, and nothing below depends on any data. The frozen registration (`gates/wind-long-range-v2.md`) is not edited; this file changes how its last sentence of section 9 ("gust results are reported, not gated") and the gust lines of sections 4 and 6 are read from now on. Named `-amendment-gust` so it cannot collide with the amendment numbers already used or reserved (1, 2, and a possible GEFSv12 fallback).

## Why
Under the registration as written, gust is reported only, so no arm can ever ship a gust from this work: "No arm outside the three gated ones can ship", and a reported-only line cannot make a gust shippable. Wind and Gusts need range parity at the long range, so gust must be tested under its own pre-registered bars or the registration can never legitimately serve one. Making it gated before TEST starts is the only legitimate moment; after TEST is read it would be a new registration on later data.

## G1. Gust is a gated metric, tested serially AFTER wind
- A new metric **MG = gust MAE (m/s)**, scored on identical rows (station, valid time, lead) beside M1 and M2.
- **Serial order (fixed now).** Step 1: the wind gates (F1, F3, G4-G6) are decided exactly as registered, untouched. Step 2: gust is TESTED only for an arm that has PASSED the wind gates for that tail (ICON tail, EURO tail). An arm that fails wind for a tail is not gust-tested there. Gust never changes a wind verdict and takes no alpha from F1, F3 or F6 (its family is separate, G4).
- **A gust fail ships that tail as WIND ONLY:** that model's Gusts layer keeps its native end and its "ends at day X" label. A gust pass ships the arm's gust for the tail (guarded flag, kill switch, SCOREBOARD row, history filter, labelled with its time window "max over the last 6 h"). Gust >= the served wind holds by construction, because both come from the same arm's `f_s` and `GF`: gust = `max(f_s, f_s * GF[b,r])`, GF interpolated in lead like the other coefficients.
- Gust arms are the same two calibrated arms, `GRAND_CAL` and `AIGEFS_CAL`. `CLIM_SERVE` has no gust. No new arm, no new coefficient. Preference per tail: `GRAND_CAL`, then `AIGEFS_CAL`, as for wind.
- **Reported on every arm and tail, including those that fail wind and are therefore not gust-tested (REPORTED ONLY, no effect on any verdict):** the unconditional gust metrics (MG, bias, the comparators below) and the lines against `WIND140` and `PERSIST`. This shows whether the condition hides gust skill or flatters it, without shipping a gust on a wind that is not served.

## G2. Gust truth (the same QC as #1063, nothing looser)
- **NDBC `GST` only** (buoys and C-MAN; the peak gust in the station's sampling period), as in `2026-11-18-opus-gust-verification.md` section 1 and amendment 1 there. ASOS gust is NOT used for gust: it is reported only when it is gusty, so missing means "no gust reported", which would bias a gust score.
- Numeric `GST` only (the NDBC missing-value sentinels are not observations). **Time window matched to the forecast gust (the 6 h window max):** the truth at valid time V is the MAX of the hourly observations (each the report nearest the hour within +-10 min) over the 6 hours ending at V, as #1063 does for a window-maximum model (the max over V-w+1..V with all w present, here w = 6, the window of IFS 10fg past 144 h and the one the Gusts layer labels "max over the last 6 h"). **All six hourly records must be present** (fixed now); a row without them, or with a missing GST, is dropped from gust lines ONLY, never filled. The dropped rows are COUNTED and reported by station and bin, and a sensitivity read with "at least 4 of 6" records is REPORTED ONLY (no verdict depends on it).
- **Height adjustment:** the neutral log law `U10 = Uz * ln(10 / z0) / ln(z / z0)`, z0 = 0.0002 m, z = site elevation + anemometer height from the current `stationmetadata.xml` entry, applied to GST and WSPD alike. **Gated rows use ADJUSTED stations only: 1 m <= z <= 50 m.** Stations with no metadata or a height outside that range are scored RAW and reported, never gated. The gust truth stations must also be marine-exposed under the registered rule (including the A7 5 km water test for buoys).
- The same truth snapshots, weekly artifact and source-recording rule as section 4 apply. TEST gust truth is NDBC realtime2 snapshots replaced by the monthly stdmet files when published; FIT gust truth is the historical stdmet files.
- **Consequence for the fit (a build change, done in PR #1192, not an arm change):** `GF` is the median of observed gust divided by calibrated speed over rows with calibrated speed >= 3 m/s. Amendment 1 A4 already replaced the registration's "member median of the arm's gust" wording with this form (one definition for all arms, AIGEFS included) and left "observed gust" open between NDBC GST and ASOS gust; this amendment fixes it to the gust truth above, so the fitted GF and the scored gust share one definition. ASOS rows are out of the gust fit and score (the build no longer treats an absent ASOS gust as the wind); the coefficient hash covers the result.

## G3. Gust arms and comparators
- **Gated comparators (fixed now):**
  - **`CLIM_GUST`:** the station-record climatology of the 6 h max GST at each station, 2016-2025, by day of year (+-15 d) and 6-hourly UTC hour, >= 20 records per cell (the `CLIM_STN` construction applied to the gust truth of G2; no ERA5 dependency). A station-cell without it is dropped from CLIM_GUST lines, reported, never filled. Built before the freeze and hashed with the other frozen artifacts (a build item, not yet built).
  - **`FIX140`:** the arm's OWN calibrated speed `f_s` x 1.40, the factor frozen at #1063's 1.40 and never refitted. It uses no stand-in wind and asks whether the fitted GF adds anything over a constant.
- **Reported-only comparators (no bar, no family, no effect on any verdict):**
  - **`WIND140`:** the comparator system's own 10 m wind times 1.40 (the `LOOP_PROXY` wind for the ICON tail, the GFS wind of the same run for the EURO tail, on the cells of wind's comparators).
  - **`PERSIST`:** the latest observed gust at the run's init time t0 by the same observation definition and adjustment. Lenient on purpose (an observation at t0 is not in hand when the run is issued).
- **Forbidden stand-ins stay forbidden.** `WIND140` is a reported baseline only. No gust factor is applied to the GFS-copy EURO wind or to the looped ICON wind in any served path from this registration, whatever the result. If a gust arm fails in a tail, the served gust in that tail is whatever it is today.

## G4. Bars and their statistic (the same family as wind's)
Statistic: paired difference of absolute error, arm minus comparator, on identical rows; moving 5-day-block bootstrap over valid dates, B = 10,000, seed 20261122, 95% percentile interval; one-sided Holm-adjusted 0.025 for a "CI upper < 0" line (section 5). Better means negative. Cells are the same five as F1: ICON tail {B1, B2, B3}; EURO tail {B2 rows with lead > 240 h, B3}.
- **ONE gust Holm family FG, 20 lines:** arm in {`GRAND_CAL`, `AIGEFS_CAL`} x [`CLIM_GUST` x 5 cells (FG1) + `FIX140` x 5 cells (FG2)]. It is adjusted over all 20 lines whether or not both arms reach it (the serial order only decides which lines are evaluated; the adjustment is conservative and fixed now). It is separate from F1, F3, F6.
- **FG1 (vs `CLIM_GUST`):** the arm must be significantly BETTER (CI upper < 0) in the B1 and B2 cells; in the B3 cells it must NOT be significantly worse (CI lower <= 0). The B3 reading mirrors G3: expected to be a draw at long range, labelled "low skill" if it does not beat `CLIM_GUST`.
- **FG2 (vs `FIX140`, non-inferiority):** the arm must NOT be significantly worse than `FIX140` (CI lower <= 0) in every cell.
- **GG4 seam (descriptive threshold check, as G4).** The p95 of the absolute gust difference between the arm's first extension step and the native model's last native gust step (EURO: HRES 10fg at 240 h, a 6 h max; ICON: read forward on >= 10 live ICON runs from STORED products) <= the p95 of within-native 6 h gust changes over the last 24 native hours; and the arm's own 6 h gust change across 216 h and 264 h must not exceed the p95 at non-edge leads. Hard hand-off, no taper.
- **GG5 replication (descriptive).** The FG1 B1/B2 lines keep their sign with a point estimate at least half the pooled size in BOTH TEST halves (first 45, last 45 runs by init time).
- **GG6 regions.** No region with sufficient gust data is Holm-significantly worse than `CLIM_GUST` on MG in any bin (Holm within each arm x bin across regions).
- An arm PASSES gust for a tail only if it passed wind for that tail, all its FG1 and FG2 lines hold, GG4, GG5 and GG6 hold, and the controls below do not misbehave.
- **Power note (reported before dispatch, never changes a bar):** the TEST span has about 10 independent 5-day blocks, so FG has low power and a gust FAIL means "not shown", not "shown no skill". Before the freeze the build reports, from the FIT rows ONLY, the SD of the paired per-block differences for each line type and the minimum detectable effect at 80% power with about 10 blocks, so the owner reads a gust fail knowing what it could not detect.

## G5. Floors and controls
- **Floors (VOID if not met, never failed):** the registration's run and day floors apply unchanged (>= 80 kept runs, >= 45 days, >= 9 blocks, <= 10% dropped). Gust adds: >= 10 marine-exposed ADJUSTED NDBC GST stations with >= 70% hourly GST records in each region counted sufficient; a region below it is "insufficient" for GG6, never a verdict. Europe has no NDBC GST stations on the registered lists, so it is expected to be insufficient and any gust ship text says "not tested in Europe". If fewer than 3 regions are sufficient for gust, the gust verdict is VOID (the wind verdict is unaffected). (The gust-labels lane's looser proposal of >= 8 stations in >= 2 regions is not adopted: looser floors make the verdict easier to pass by chance.)
- **Controls (a misbehaving control makes the GUST verdict VOID only):** the section 7 controls on gust rows: null (an arm identical to `FIX140` gives exactly zero difference with a zero-width CI), shuffled pairing (k = 7 days: the arm's gain over `CLIM_GUST` disappears in B1 and B2), shifted valid time (+48 h must give a higher MG in B1 and B2), plant (+1 m/s on the arm's gust must show a signed-bias difference whose CI contains +1.0 and excludes 0 in every bin), and the calibration-leak control (coefficient hash including GF matches; FIT and TEST share no (init date, station)).
- Reported only, never gating: gust bias by bin, RAW versus ADJUSTED gap, the dense-window sensitivity, Brier score for gust >= 15 m/s (UNDERPOWERED below 30 observed events), the land/sea split, and the unconditional lines of G1 and the reported-only comparators of G3.

## G7. What this changes and does not change
- Changes: gust gets a serial test after wind, two gated comparators (`CLIM_GUST`, `FIX140`), one Holm family (FG, 20 lines), a seam check, floors and controls, all fixed now; the gust truth is fixed to ADJUSTED NDBC GST over a 6 h window; `WIND140` and `PERSIST` and the unconditional gust metrics are reported.
- Does not change: any wind line, bar, family, floor or control; the arms; the freeze content except that the GF coefficients are fitted on the gust truth defined here and `CLIM_GUST` is added to the hashed artifacts; the stop rule; the blinding; no dispatch without the owner's typed word; no served number moves from this file.
