# Amendment G1 to gate wind-long-range-v2: gust becomes a GATED metric (before any TEST data exists or is read)
Date: 2026-11-22. Made by: the build session (`local_318ba0a1`, Claude), at the director's request (`local_d6b521eb`, relaying the gust lanes' finding). To be sent to the advisor BEFORE any dispatch and BEFORE the TEST freeze (first TEST init 2026-12-01 00Z). No TEST row exists, no FIT or TEST gust value has been collected or read, and nothing below depends on any data. The frozen registration (`gates/wind-long-range-v2.md`) is not edited; this file changes how its last sentence of section 9 ("gust results are reported, not gated") and the gust lines of sections 4 and 6 are read from now on. Named `-amendment-gust` so it cannot collide with the amendment numbers already used or reserved (1, 2, and a possible GEFSv12 fallback).

## Why
Under the registration as written, gust is reported only, so no arm can ever ship a gust from this work: "No arm outside the three gated ones can ship", and a reported-only line cannot make a gust shippable. Wind and Gusts need range parity at the long range, so gust must be tested under its own pre-registered bars or the registration can never legitimately serve one. Making it gated before TEST starts is the only legitimate moment; after TEST is read it would be a new registration on later data.

## G1. Gust is a separate gated metric, judged independently of wind
- A new metric **MG = gust MAE (m/s)**, scored on identical rows (station, valid time, lead) beside M1 and M2. Wind verdicts (G1-G6) are unchanged and do not depend on gust; **gust verdicts do not depend on wind**. Each tail (ICON, EURO) gets its own gust verdict per arm.
- Gated gust arms are the same two calibrated arms, `GRAND_CAL` and `AIGEFS_CAL`, with the gust the registration already defines: `max(f_s, f_s * GF[b,r])`, GF interpolated in lead like the other coefficients. `CLIM_SERVE` has no gust and is not a gust arm. No new arm, no new coefficient, no new comparator source is added.
- Preference per tail when a gust arm passes: `GRAND_CAL`, then `AIGEFS_CAL`, the same order as wind. A gust arm that fails ships nothing for that tail from this registration.

## G2. Gust truth (the same QC as #1063, nothing looser)
- **NDBC `GST` only** (buoys and C-MAN; the peak gust in the station's sampling period), as in `2026-11-18-opus-gust-verification.md` section 1 and amendment 1 there. ASOS gust is NOT used for gust: it is reported only when it is gusty, so missing means "no gust reported", which would bias a gust score.
- Numeric `GST` only (the NDBC missing-value sentinels are not observations). **Time window matched to the forecast gust (the 6 h window max):** the truth at valid time V is the MAX of the hourly observations (each the report nearest the hour within +-10 min) over the 6 hours ending at V, as #1063 does for a window-maximum model (the max over V-w+1..V with all w present, here w = 6, the window of IFS 10fg past 144 h and the one the Gusts layer labels "max over the last 6 h"). All six hourly records must be present, as in #1063; a row without them, or with a missing GST, is dropped from gust lines ONLY (counted and reported by station and bin), and never filled. Whether a looser "at least 4 of 6" rule should replace "all 6" is an open point (G6.5), to be set from the freeze-time station count, never from a score.
- **Height adjustment:** the neutral log law `U10 = Uz * ln(10 / z0) / ln(z / z0)`, z0 = 0.0002 m, z = site elevation + anemometer height from the current `stationmetadata.xml` entry, applied to GST and WSPD alike. **Gated rows use ADJUSTED stations only: 1 m <= z <= 50 m.** Stations with no metadata or a height outside that range are scored RAW and reported, never gated. The gust truth stations must also be marine-exposed under the registered rule (including the A7 5 km water test for buoys).
- The same truth snapshots, weekly artifact and source-recording rule as section 4 apply. TEST gust truth is NDBC realtime2 snapshots replaced by the monthly stdmet files when published; FIT gust truth is the historical stdmet files.
- **Consequence for the fit (a build change, flagged, not an arm change):** `GF` is already defined on FIT as the median of observed gust divided by calibrated speed over rows with calibrated speed >= 3 m/s. Amendment 1 A4 already replaced the registration's "member median of the arm's gust" wording with this observed-gust-over-calibrated-speed form (one definition for all arms, AIGEFS included), and it left "observed gust" open between NDBC GST and ASOS gust; this amendment fixes it to the gust truth above (the 6 h window max at ADJUSTED NDBC stations), so the fitted GF and the scored gust share one definition. In particular the FIT rows must NOT treat an absent ASOS gust as the wind (the build currently does for ASOS rows, which censors gust truth low): ASOS rows are out of the gust fit and the gust score. The truth reader and fit are changed to match in a build PR before the freeze; the coefficient hash covers the result.

## G3. Gust arms and comparators
- **Arms:** `GRAND_CAL` gust and `AIGEFS_CAL` gust (above).
- **Comparator `WIND140`:** the comparator system's own 10 m wind speed at the same station, valid time and lead, times **1.40**, fixed now (the baseline #1063's scoreboard row shows beats every raw model gust in every lead bin but one). For the ICON tail the wind is the `LOOP_PROXY` wind; for the EURO tail it is the GFS wind of the same run, on the same rows and cells as wind's comparators (`LOOP_PROXY` x {B1, B2, B3}; `GFS` x {B2 rows with lead > 240 h, B3}).
- **Comparator `FIX140` (suggested by the gust-horizon lane's draft):** the arm's OWN calibrated speed `f_s` x 1.40, the factor frozen at #1063's 1.40 and never refitted. It uses no stand-in wind. It asks whether the fitted GF adds anything over a constant. Tested non-inferiority only (below).
- **Comparator `PERSIST`:** the latest observed gust at the run's init time t0, by the same observation definition and the same adjustment (the hourly GST at t0). Lenient on purpose: an observation at t0 is not in hand when the run is issued (as in #1063).
- **Forbidden stand-ins stay forbidden.** `WIND140` is a SCORING baseline only. It is not an arm, it cannot ship, and no gust factor is applied to the GFS-copy EURO wind or to the looped ICON wind in any served path from this registration, whatever the result. If a gust arm fails in a tail, the served gust in that tail is whatever it is today, not a factor on a stand-in.

## G4. Bars and their statistic (the same family as wind's)
Statistic: paired difference of absolute error, arm minus comparator, on identical rows; moving 5-day-block bootstrap over valid dates, B = 10,000, seed 20261122, 95% percentile interval; one-sided Holm-adjusted 0.025 for "CI upper < 0" (section 5). Better means negative.
- **Family FG (gust speed):** arm in {`GRAND_CAL`, `AIGEFS_CAL`} x comparator in {`WIND140`, `PERSIST`} x the five comparator-bin cells = 2 x 2 x 5 = **20 lines**, one Holm family. (The cells are the same five as F1.)
- **Family FG2 (non-inferior to a constant factor):** arm minus `FIX140` on MG, 2 arms x 5 cells = **10 lines**, a second one-sided Holm family. **GG2.** On every FG2 line of the arm the arm is NOT significantly worse than `FIX140` (CI lower <= 0).
- **GG1.** CI upper < 0 on EVERY FG line of the arm (so it must beat `WIND140` and `PERSIST` in every cell).
- **GG4 seam.** The same rule as G4 applied to GUST: the p95 of the absolute gust difference between the arm's first extension step and the native model's last native gust step (EURO: HRES 10fg at 240 h; ICON: read forward on >= 10 live ICON runs from STORED products) <= the p95 of within-native 3 h gust changes over the last 24 native hours; and the arm's own 6 h gust change across 216 h and 264 h must not exceed the p95 at non-edge leads. Hard hand-off, no taper.
- **GG5 replication.** The same sign and a point estimate at least half the pooled size in BOTH TEST halves (first 45, last 45 runs by init time), for each FG line the arm needs; descriptive, no p-value.
- **GG6 regions.** No region with sufficient gust data is Holm-significantly worse than `WIND140` on MG in any bin (Holm within each arm x bin across regions), as G6.
- **No GG3.** There is no gust climatology in this registration, so no "versus climatology" line exists for gust. It is not replaced by a looser bar.
- An arm PASSES gust only if GG1 (all 10 of its own FG lines, out of the family's 20), GG2 (its 5 FG2 lines), GG4, GG5 and GG6 hold and the controls below do not misbehave.

## G5. Floors and controls
- **Floors (VOID if not met, never failed):** the registration's run and day floors apply unchanged (>= 80 kept runs, >= 45 days, >= 9 blocks, <= 10% dropped). Gust adds: >= 10 marine-exposed ADJUSTED NDBC GST stations with >= 70% hourly GST records in each region counted sufficient; a region below it is "insufficient" for GG6, never a verdict. Europe has no NDBC GST stations on the registered lists, so it is expected to be insufficient for gust and the Europe half of any gust ship is stated as "not tested in Europe" in the ship text. If fewer than 3 regions are sufficient for gust, the gust verdict is VOID (the wind verdict is unaffected).
- **Controls (VOID of the gust verdict only if any misbehaves):** the section 7 controls are run on gust rows: null (an arm equal to the comparator gives exactly zero), shuffled pairing (the arm's gain over `WIND140` disappears under the registered 7-day shuffle: the CI of arm minus `WIND140` includes 0 or is positive in B1 and B2), shifted valid time (+48 h must give a higher MG in B1 and B2), plant (+1 m/s on the arm's gust must show a signed-bias difference whose CI contains +1.0 and excludes 0 in every bin), and the calibration-leak control (the coefficient hash, now including GF, matches; FIT and TEST share no (init date, station)). A VOID gust control does not void the wind verdict.
- Reported only, never gating: gust bias by bin, RAW versus ADJUSTED gap, the dense-window sensitivity, Brier score for gust >= 15 m/s (UNDERPOWERED below 30 observed events), and the land/sea split.

## G6. Open points for the advisor (stated so a gap cannot read as a pass)
1. **Reading of the forbidden stand-ins.** `WIND140` uses the looped ICON wind and the GFS-copy EURO wind as a SCORING baseline only (the comparison that decides whether a calibrated gust is worth serving). If the advisor reads "no gust factor on those winds" as also barring them as a comparator, the comparator becomes the raw native gust field where the comparator system serves one, and this paragraph is amended before dispatch.
2. **Serving a gust without its wind.** Because wind and gust verdicts are independent, a tail could pass gust and fail wind. The served pair would then need gust >= the served wind in that tail. Whether a gust-only ship is allowed, and how the pair is kept consistent, is NOT decided here; it is a build-PR check and an owner decision before any ship.
3. **ICON gust seam** is read forward on live ICON runs (as for wind) and is pending until those runs exist.
4. **Licence/serving terms** for the AIGEFS-containing gust arm are the same open legal read as wind's (section 9).
5. **Two competing drafts, one to land.** The gust-horizon lane drafted a different A7 (gust gated ONLY for an arm that already passes wind; comparators `CLIM_GUST` station climatology and `FIX140`; floors >= 8 stations in >= 2 regions; "at least 4 of 6" records). This amendment follows the director's instruction (gust judged independently of wind; comparators `WIND140` and `PERSIST`) and takes from that draft the 6 h window-max truth, `FIX140` as a non-inferiority bar, and the ASOS exclusion. The unresolved choices are (a) independent versus conditional on wind, (b) `CLIM_GUST` as an extra bar, (c) the "4 of 6" rule and the floors; the director and advisor decide before dispatch.

## G7. What this changes and does not change
- Changes: gust gets bars, a comparator set, a Holm family (FG, 20 lines), a seam check, floors and controls, all fixed now; the gust truth is fixed to ADJUSTED NDBC GST.
- Does not change: any wind line, bar, family, floor or control; the arms; the freeze content except that the GF coefficients are fitted on the gust truth defined here; the stop rule; the blinding; no dispatch without the owner's typed word; no served number moves from this file.
