# Amendment 1 to currents-skill-v1 (build rulings)
Stamp: 2026-11-22. These are the advisor's rulings on the build's amendment questions A1-A12, as relayed by the director on PR #1153, gate HOLD at `626b37680`. They were written before any TEST data exists, and nothing has been collected. Where this file and `currents-skill-v1.md` differ, this file holds. Every other rule is unchanged.

| # | Question | Ruling | Where it is built |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Which frames are scored | **Accepted.** Score the served frames as served. When a frame is one-byte, the vector is the node's 0.02 kn speed band along its 2 × 2 block's direction, in 5.625° steps. **The quantisation is reported:** the result lists each arm's encodings (`quantisation`). | `currents_skill_arms.decode_frame_uv`, report `quantisation` |
| A2 | First scored step | **Accepted.** Every arm is scored from its 6 h step, and PERSIST holds the 6 h step. The 0 h steps of CMEMS and RTOFS are ignored. | `arms.FIRST_STEP_H` |
| A3 | Start of M2's filtered field | **Changed.** The window is not truncated: the registered ≥ 20-of-25 rule holds for every arm's field and for the truth track. M2 seeds after the init instead. The ruling said +12 h, but the steps start at 6 h, so the 25 h window at +12 h (hours 0-24) holds only 19 defined hours (6-24). M2 therefore seeds at **+13 h**, the first lead that meets the rule, and is scored 72 h later at +85 h. The truth seed and end are the smoothed positions at those hours. | `metrics.M2_SEED_H = 13`; `arms.lowpassed_velocity_fn(need=20)` |
| A4 | Zone precedence | **Accepted.** First match wins, in the listed order: COAST50 > SHELF > WBC > EQ > OPEN. | `currents_skill_geo.zone_of` |
| A5 | Bathymetry | **Changed.** Use ETOPO 2022 15″ as registered, by 1° tiles from ERDDAP `ETOPO_2022_v1_15s` (CC0), fetched once and cached. Land is z > 0 (the wind-lr2 `EtopoMask` rule). COAST50 is the distance to the nearest land cell; depth is −z. The ETOPO1 0.25° asset is not used, because it misplaces the 50 km line by up to about 14 km. | `geo.Etopo2022`; score `--etopo-cache` |
| A6 | Drifter hours and QC | **Accepted.** QC flag 1 only. Fixes are binned to the nearest hour and gaps are never interpolated. **The drifter-inits excluded by the 20 h rule are reported:** `drifter_inits_excluded_20h_rule` counts tracks with fixes in the span that fail the rule. | `truth.bin_hourly`, `metrics.m2_rows(stats=)` |
| A7 | COMPARABLE versus WORSE | **Accepted as built.** The precedence is BETTER > COMPARABLE > WORSE, with `worse_significant` beside every label. A COMPARABLE cell whose worse line also holds is **worded "within 10%, measurably worse"**. | `stats.label_family` (`wording`) |
| A8 | Collection horizon | **Accepted.** Collect to 180 h. The D7 window needs steps to 180 h. | `collect.MAX_HOUR` |
| A9 | Dataset IDs | **Accepted.** Use `ucsdHfrW6_Lon0360` and `ucsdHfrE6_Lon0360`; the Puerto Rico / USVI mirror `ucsdHfrP6` reads `hfradar_prvi_6km`. **The Hawaii ID (PacIOOS `hfradar_ushi_6km`) is fixed at freeze** and recorded in the result's first line. | `collect.REGIONS` |
| A10 | Arm for the control plants | **Changed.** The speed and direction plants go on **GIOPS_025 if it has rows**, else on CMEMS_083, else on CMEMS_025 compared with its own unplanted self. This keeps the controls evaluable while GIOPS is dark. The arm used is recorded as `plant_arm`. | `score.plant_arm` |
| A11 | Collection cadence | **Owner decision (the director is asking David).** Both forms are built, and nothing runs without his word. (a) Manual: a dispatch at least every other day. (b) Optional schedule: one daily cron, which runs only while `CURRENTS_SKILL_SCHEDULE=1`, `CURRENTS_SKILL_OWNER_WORD_REF` names where his word was given, and today ≤ `CURRENTS_SKILL_UNTIL`. All three are unset by default, so scheduled runs skip. The schedule collects frames + native daily, and the HF-radar snapshot + drifters on Mondays. | `.github/workflows/currents-skill-collect.yml` |
| A12 | CMEMS_083 under M2 | **Accepted.** A drifter seeded outside the three native boxes is missing for CMEMS_083, not STILL. Its M2 zones may be insufficient. | `metrics.m2_rows` |

**Gating.** None of these rulings looks at data, and none changes a margin, alpha, floor or family. A3 moves the M2 span to +13 → +85 h. That is still a 72 h separation, and its bootstrap date is the valid date at +85 h.
