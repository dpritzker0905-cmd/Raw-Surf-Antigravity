## 1. Question

## 2. Arms (all scored on the identical row set)
**Systems (inputs).** Wind 10u/10v at 6-hourly valid times; gust where the system has it.
- `IFS`: ECMWF IFS ENS, cf + 50 pf (51), 0.25 deg open data (AWS `ecmwf-forecasts`, with the GCS `ecmwf-open-data` copy under the sameness check used before). 10fg available.
- `GEFS`: NOAA GEFS 31 members, 0.25 deg (`noaa-gefs-pds`, `gefs.YYYYMMDD/HH/atmos/pgrb2sp25`, 6-hourly past 240 h as published). Gust from `GUST`.
- `AIGEFS`: NOAA AIGEFS, 31 members (`mem000`-`mem030`), 10 m UGRD/VGRD, 6-hourly, f000-f384 (bucket `noaa-nws-graphcastgfs-pds`, prefix `EAGLE_ensemble/aigefs.YYYYMMDD/HH/`). No gust field: gust = max(speed x GF, speed), GF fitted on FIT (as the GEFS and AIFS arms did before).
- Climatology `CLIM`: seasonal climatology of 10 m u, v and speed by day of year (+-15 d window) and 6-hourly UTC hour, computed from ERA5 (Copernicus C3S) over 1991-2020, bilinear at the station. Reported beside it: `CLIM_STN` (station-record climatology, 2016-2025, >= 20 records per cell, as before).

**Calibration form (the same for every calibrated arm).** For member-pooled mean vector `m` (and mean speed `s`) of the arm's systems, climatology vector `c` (and `c_s`), lead bin `b`, region `r`:
- vector: `f = c + alpha[b,r] * (m - c)`; speed: `f_s = c_s + beta[b,r] * (s - c_s) + gamma[b,r]`;
- served `(u, v)` = `f` rescaled to speed `f_s`; direction from `f`;
- gust = `max(f_s, f_s * GF[b,r])` where GF is fitted on FIT from the member median of the arm's gust (IFS, GEFS) or speed (AIGEFS);
- coefficients fitted per (bin x region) by ridge regression of station truth on the pooled arm, shrunk toward `(alpha, beta, gamma) = (1, 1, 0)`, with the ridge strength chosen by leave-block-out cross-validation INSIDE FIT only. **The shrinkage target is the RAW pooled field (alpha = 1), NOT climatology (alpha = 0). This is intended:** the fit may only move away from the model where FIT evidence supports it, so a cell with thin evidence stays the raw pool (a conservative prior about changing the model). The consequence is stated openly: in such cells the arm is the raw pool, and the reported `GRAND_RAW` / `IFS_RAW` arms show what that costs. The cross-validation grid includes lambda = 0 and lambda = infinity (raw), so the data can choose either extreme;
- **smooth in lead (no new seams at the bin edges).** The fitted `(alpha, beta, gamma, GF)` belong to the bin CENTRES (B1 198 h, B2 240 h, B3 300 h) and are linearly interpolated in lead between centres, held flat below 198 h and above 300 h. The step-free field is what is scored and served; bin edges are reporting boundaries only;
- `m` and `s` are plain equal-weight pools over all members of the arm's systems (system weights equal, fixed now, never fitted).
Regions r: US Pacific, US Atlantic + Gulf, Hawaii, Europe Atlantic, Mediterranean (the 3b region list). A region with too few FIT rows (rule in section 5) borrows the pooled coefficients, flagged.

**Gated arms (three, fixed now):**
- `GRAND_CAL`: IFS + GEFS + AIGEFS pooled, calibrated. PRIMARY.
- `AIGEFS_CAL`: AIGEFS alone, calibrated.
- `CLIM_SERVE` (owner decision 2026-11-22, relayed by the director: "move forward on the best SOTA path"): the `CLIM` field above, served as-is and LABELLED as climatology wherever it is served, never presented as a forecast. It is the same frozen field as the reported `CLIM` (ERA5, no station fitting, no coefficients; its frozen artifact is the climatology file already hashed in section 3). It exists so that past the useful horizon the product can say honestly "typical wind for this date" instead of a loop or a clone. It is gated on G1, G2, G4, G5 and G6; **G3 does not apply to it** (it cannot beat itself). **Gatekeeping:** `CLIM_SERVE` is tested only for a tail where BOTH calibrated arms have failed (section 5), so the calibrated arms keep their full-family power. Passing means what G1 and G2 say for every arm: significantly better than the comparator (CI upper < 0, Holm-adjusted) on M1 and M2 in every bin, plus G4, G5 and G6. It does not mean "has forecast skill".

**Reported-only arms (no gate, no effect on the verdict):** `IFS_RAW` (the #939 arm, as a reference: raw mean of member speeds), `IFS_CAL`, `GEFS_CAL`, `GRAND_RAW` (uncalibrated pool), `CLIM_STN`, `LAG24` (previous-cycle pooled mean at L+24). (`CLIM` is reported as the G3 reference; as an arm it is `CLIM_SERVE`.)

**Comparators (served today, scored where each is served):**
- `LOOP_PROXY` for the ICON tail, leads 180-336 h: rebuilt from the ECMWF HRES run's own native whole days with the `extend_icon_wind_to_14d` rule (lead L replays lead `L mod 168`). Declared a PROXY: ICON has no public archive; its skill comes from replaying a different valid day, not from the model's identity.

## 5. Statistics and floors
- Paired differences on identical (station, valid time, lead) rows.
- Moving-block bootstrap over VALID DATES: blocks of 5 days, B = 10,000, seed 20261122, 95% percentile interval. TEST valid span ~52 days gives about 10 blocks; the result states this is FRAGILE (few independent synoptic regimes at 8-14 d) and a FAIL means "not shown", not "shown no skill".
- **Test lines and Holm families (every tested line is named; each belongs to exactly one family).** A line is one-sided: arm minus comparator, "better" meaning negative. Its Holm-adjusted p-value comes from the same bootstrap; "CI upper < 0" in section 6 means the line is significant at Holm-adjusted one-sided 0.025 (equivalent to the 95% two-sided interval used for reporting). G4 and G5 are descriptive threshold checks, not hypothesis tests, and carry no p-values.
  - **F1 (G1 + G2):** arm in {GRAND_CAL, AIGEFS_CAL} x metric in {M1, M2} x comparator-bin cell. Comparator-bin cells: `LOOP_PROXY` x {B1, B2, B3} (3) and `GFS` x {B2 rows with lead > 240 h, B3} (2) = 5 cells. Total 2 x 2 x 5 = **20 lines**, one Holm family.
  - **Gatekeeping order for `CLIM_SERVE` (fixed now).** Step 1: F1, F3, G4-G6 are evaluated for the two calibrated arms exactly as above. Step 2: only for a tail (ICON or EURO) where neither calibrated arm passes, `CLIM_SERVE` is tested for that tail in its own family **F1c: 2 metrics x that tail's comparator-bin cells** (ICON tail: `LOOP_PROXY` x 3 bins = 6 lines; EURO tail: `GFS` x 2 cells = 4 lines), Holm within F1c. `CLIM_SERVE`'s G6 lines form their own Holm set the same way. For a tail where a calibrated arm passes, `CLIM_SERVE` is not tested and its lines are reported descriptively only. No line of `CLIM_SERVE` enters F1, F3 or the calibrated arms' F6.
  - **F3 (G3):** calibrated arm (`GRAND_CAL`, `AIGEFS_CAL`; NOT `CLIM_SERVE`) x {B1 beat `CLIM`, B2 beat `CLIM`, B3 not worse than `CLIM`} on M1 = 2 x 3 = **6 lines**, one Holm family. (The B3 line is the reverse-direction test, "arm minus `CLIM` is not significantly positive".)
  - **F6 (G6):** calibrated arm x region (up to 5, those with sufficient data) x bin (3) x comparator (`LOOP_PROXY`; plus `GFS` on B2-above-240 and B3 rows) on M1, with the direction "arm significantly WORSE than the comparator", Holm within each (arm, bin, comparator) across regions. Reported as the number of regions flagged.
  - Controls (section 7) are not Holm families; each has its own pass rule.
  - An arm passes only if all its lines in F1 and F3 are significant (or, for the B3 line, not rejected) after adjustment, and the G4-G6 checks hold.
- **Floors (VOID if not met, never failed):** >= 80 kept TEST runs, >= 45 distinct valid days, >= 9 five-day blocks, >= 10 marine-exposed stations with >= 70% hourly records in each of >= 3 regions (US Pacific, US Atlantic + Gulf, Hawaii are the expected three; a region below its minimum is "insufficient" for G6, never a verdict). More than 10% of the 90 TEST runs dropped after pass 2 also VOIDS. FIT floor: >= 60 kept FIT runs and >= 100 station-days per (bin x region) cell, else that cell borrows pooled coefficients, flagged.

## 6. Pass rules (ALL must hold for an arm to be shipped; each arm tested separately; Holm-adjusted)
- **G1 speed.** Arm minus comparator, M1: CI upper < 0 in EVERY bin, versus `LOOP_PROXY` (leads 180-336) and versus `GFS` (only rows with lead > 240 h are compared against `GFS`, so that line covers the part of B2 above 240 h and all of B3; a bin with no such rows is skipped for that comparator).
- **G2 wind factor.** The same lines for M2.
- **G3 versus climatology (calibrated arms only; not applied to `CLIM_SERVE`).** Arm minus `CLIM`, M1: CI upper < 0 in B1 AND B2. In B3 the arm must NOT be significantly worse (CI lower <= 0); B3 is labelled "low skill" if the arm does not beat `CLIM` there. (The shrinkage form tends to `CLIM` as skill decays, so this is expected to be a draw in B3, not a hidden fallback.)
- **G4 seam.** Hard hand-off, no taper (owner, 2026-11-15). At the station cells, the p95 over cell-runs of the absolute speed difference between the arm's first extension step and the native model's last native step is <= the p95 of within-native 3 h changes over the last 24 native hours of the same runs and cells (EURO: HRES at 240 h; ICON: no archive, so ICON's seam is read forward on >= 10 live ICON runs from STORED products before the ICON half ships). **Edges inside the arm.** Because the coefficients are interpolated in lead (section 2), the 216 h and 264 h bin edges carry no coefficient step by construction; this is CHECKED, not assumed: the same p95 rule is applied at both edges, comparing the arm's own 6 h speed change across 216 h and across 264 h (valid-time step, same run and cell) against the p95 of the arm's own 6 h changes at non-edge leads of the same runs and cells (<=). The native seam (180 h / 240 h) and both edges must all hold. If G4 fails, the arm does not ship as registered; a taper is a NEW decision for the owner, not an amendment. The hard hand-off itself (no taper, no offset, owner 2026-11-15) is unchanged.
- **G5 replication (applies to all three gated arms, `CLIM_SERVE` included).** The same sign and a point estimate at least half the pooled size in BOTH halves of TEST (the first 45 and the last 45 runs by init time; descriptive check, no p-value, no family).
- **G6 regions.** No region with sufficient data is Holm-significantly worse than the comparator on M1 in any bin (the 3b report-by-region habit: opposite signs by coast have happened before).
- **Choosing what ships.** Order of preference, per tail: `GRAND_CAL`, then `AIGEFS_CAL`, then `CLIM_SERVE`. A calibrated arm that passes G1-G6 ships and `CLIM_SERVE` does not (the calibrated arm has shown it beats climatology); `AIGEFS_CAL` ships only if `GRAND_CAL` fails and `AIGEFS_CAL` passes alone; `CLIM_SERVE` ships, labelled as climatology ("typical for this date", never "forecast"), only for a tail where no calibrated arm passes and `CLIM_SERVE` (tested under the section 5 gatekeeping order) passes G1, G2, G4, G5 and G6. Per tail: the ICON half and the EURO half are decided on their own comparator lines; either can pass alone, and the two tails can ship different arms. The label is part of the shipped arm and is checked in the build PR. No arm outside the three gated ones can ship from this registration, whatever the reported-only lines show.

## 7. Controls (VOID if any misbehaves)
- **Null.** An arm identical to the comparator gives exactly zero difference with a zero-width CI.
- **Shuffled pairing.** The arm's forecast for init `t` paired with truth of the matching valid hour at init `t + k days` (k fixed at 7, within TEST): the gain over `CLIM` must disappear (95% CI of arm minus `CLIM` includes 0 or is positive) in B1 and B2. If a shuffled pairing still beats `CLIM` with an excluded-zero CI, the instrument leaks and the run is VOID. (This replaces the old "+48 h shift must lose to climatology", which a shrunk arm that sits at `CLIM` in B3 cannot satisfy by construction.)
- **Shifted valid time.** The arm scored against truth at V + 48 h must have a HIGHER M1 than the unshifted arm in B1 and B2 (point estimate). Else VOID.
- **Plant, speed.** +1 m/s added to the arm: signed-bias difference vs the unseeded arm has a CI containing +1.0 and excluding 0, in every bin. Else VOID.
- **Plants, direction.** Wind direction rotated +30 deg (1x) and +60 deg (2x) on the same random 15% of TEST rows (seed 20261122): the signed-direction ME test in the PLANT family catches the 2x plant; the 1x plant is reported. If the 2x plant is not caught, VOID.
- **Calibration-leak control.** The coefficient files' sha256 equals the committed value, and the FIT run list shares no (init date, station) with TEST. Else VOID.
