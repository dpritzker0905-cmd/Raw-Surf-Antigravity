## A1. GEFS source is the 0.5 deg file set, for every lead (registration section 2 said 0.25 deg)
Found by listing the public bucket (no forecast value read): the 0.25 deg `pgrb2sp25` member files exist only to f240 (000-240 at 3 h; `...f246` and later return 404). The 0.5 deg `pgrb2ap5` files carry every 6 h step past 240 h with `UGRD`/`VGRD` 10 m above ground (control `gec00` included), and `GUST` is in `pgrb2bp5`. So the registration's phrase "0.25 deg, 6-hourly past 240 h as published" cannot be done. Reading: GEFS = `noaa-gefs-pds` `pgrb2ap5` (u, v) and `pgrb2bp5` (gust), members `gec00` and `gep01`..`gep30`, all scored leads, bilinear at the station. One grid for all leads, so no resolution step inside the bin range. Effect on gates: none in form; the GEFS contribution to `GRAND_CAL` is coarser than registered.

## A2. CLIM is built from ERA5 monthly means by hour of day, interpolated in day of year
Registration: ERA5 1991-2020 "by day of year (+-15 d window) and 6-hourly UTC hour". Same intent (a smooth seasonal mean per UTC hour), 12 x 24 fields per variable instead of 30 years of hourly fields. `c_s` is the mean SPEED (not the length of the mean vector). ## A4. Fit details the registration left open
- Ridge scale: penalty `lambda * Sxx * (theta - theta0)^2` (Sxx the sum of squares of the regressor, so `lambda` is dimensionless; the intercept `gamma` is penalised on the same scale). Grid `[0, .01, .03, .1, .3, 1, 3, 10, inf]`; `0` is least squares, `inf` is the raw pool. Selection: leave-block-out cross-validation inside FIT, folds of whole 5-day valid blocks dealt round-robin (no RNG); a tie goes to the larger lambda; a single block cannot validate and stays raw.
- Vector `alpha` and speed `(beta, gamma)` are chosen separately.
- `GF` is the median over FIT rows (calibrated speed >= 3 m/s) of observed gust divided by calibrated speed, floored at 1. The registration worded it from the member median of the arm's own gust; that definition is replaced because gust is reported, not gated, and one definition serves all arms including AIGEFS (no gust field).
- Collected steps are 180, 186, ..., 336 (27 steps). 180 and 240 bracket the native seams.

## A6. Readings the code had to choose (each is a reading of the frozen text, not a change of it)
- **Pooling:** `m` and `s` of a multi-system arm are the mean of the systems' own means (equal SYSTEM weights, per "system weights equal, fixed now"), not an equal-member pool (which would weight IFS 51/113). If the advisor reads the registration as equal member weights, say so before collection.
- **Native yardstick for G4:** collected native steps are 6-hourly, so the "within-native 3 h changes over the last 24 native hours" are the 6 h changes 216 to 240 h of HRES. The seam is |arm at 246 h - HRES at 240 h| at marine-exposed cells, p95 against that yardstick. ICON's seam is not measurable here and a tail that passes everything else is reported `PASS_PENDING_ICON_SEAM`.
- **Bin-edge check:** the arm's own 6 h speed changes at 210->216, 216->222, 258->264 and 264->270 h against its 6 h changes at all other lead pairs of the same runs and cells (p95 <=).
- **Direction plant "caught":** the 2x (60 deg) plant is caught when the mean signed direction error of the planted arm minus the unplanted arm is >= 4.5 deg (half the expected 0.15 x 60 = 9 deg) with a bootstrap CI above 0, over rows with observed speed >= 3 m/s. The 1x plant is reported.
- **Plant, speed:** the +1 m/s plant's signed-error difference has CI containing 1.0 and excluding 0 in every bin.
- **Marine-exposed:** NDBC `type = buoy` always qualifies; otherwise within 1 km of the ETOPO shoreline and rays every 10 deg across the paired spot's shore normal +-60 deg all water to 5 km. No paired spot means not marine-exposed. ETOPO windows are +-0.3 deg boxes at the station (25 km pairing reach fits inside).
- **Snapshots:** the earliest snapshot that holds a record wins; a published monthly stdmet record replaces it.
- **Reported-only arm `LAG24`** is not built (it needs the previous cycle at L+24); it is reported-only and does not affect any verdict. `IFS_RAW`, `IFS_CAL`, `GEFS_CAL`, `GRAND_RAW` are built.
- **G6 stations:** a region needs >= 10 marine-exposed stations in the bin's rows for its line; fewer is listed as insufficient.
