## A1. GEFS source is the 0.5 deg file set, for every lead (registration section 2 said 0.25 deg)
Found by listing the public bucket (no forecast value read): the 0.25 deg `pgrb2sp25` member files exist only to f240 (000-240 at 3 h; `...f246` and later return 404). The 0.5 deg `pgrb2ap5` files carry every 6 h step past 240 h with `UGRD`/`VGRD` 10 m above ground (control `gec00` included), and `GUST` is in `pgrb2bp5`. So the registration's phrase "0.25 deg, 6-hourly past 240 h as published" cannot be done. Reading: GEFS = `noaa-gefs-pds` `pgrb2ap5` (u, v) and `pgrb2bp5` (gust), members `gec00` and `gep01`..`gep30`, all scored leads, bilinear at the station. One grid for all leads, so no resolution step inside the bin range. Effect on gates: none in form; the GEFS contribution to `GRAND_CAL` is coarser than registered.

## A2. CLIM is built from ERA5 monthly means by hour of day, interpolated in day of year
Registration: ERA5 1991-2020 "by day of year (+-15 d window) and 6-hourly UTC hour". Reading: the CDS product `reanalysis-era5-single-levels-monthly-means` (`monthly_averaged_reanalysis_by_hour_of_day`), 1991-2020, variables `10m_u_component_of_wind`, `10m_v_component_of_wind`, `10m_wind_speed`; each month's mean at each UTC hour placed at mid-month and interpolated linearly in day of year (circular). Same intent (a smooth seasonal mean per UTC hour), 12 x 24 fields per variable instead of 30 years of hourly fields. `c_s` is the mean SPEED (not the length of the mean vector). Not yet exercised: this build session has no `CDS_API_KEY`, and the availability of `10m_wind_speed` in the by-hour product is unverified. **If a CDS request shows it missing, or CDS is unavailable, collection does not start; a further dated amendment goes to the advisor first** (the registered GEFSv12 fallback also needs one). Attribution is carried as registered.

## A4. Fit details the registration left open
- Ridge scale: penalty `lambda * Sxx * (theta - theta0)^2` (Sxx the sum of squares of the regressor, so `lambda` is dimensionless; the intercept `gamma` is penalised on the same scale). Grid `[0, .01, .03, .1, .3, 1, 3, 10, inf]`; `0` is least squares, `inf` is the raw pool. Selection: leave-block-out cross-validation inside FIT, folds of whole 5-day valid blocks dealt round-robin (no RNG); a tie goes to the larger lambda; a single block cannot validate and stays raw.
- Vector `alpha` and speed `(beta, gamma)` are chosen separately.
- `GF` is the median over FIT rows (calibrated speed >= 3 m/s) of observed gust divided by calibrated speed, floored at 1. The registration worded it from the member median of the arm's own gust; that definition is replaced because gust is reported, not gated, and one definition serves all arms including AIGEFS (no gust field).
- Collected steps are 180, 186, ..., 336 (27 steps). 180 and 240 bracket the native seams.
