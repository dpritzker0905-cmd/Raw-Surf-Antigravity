# The 255° residual, taken apart at one coordinate — it is NOT a mask defect

Single coordinate: **32.69350, −17.12638** (Madeira SW, the bearing no lever ever moved).
Harnesses: `frontend/scripts/teardown-255{,-ladder,-whatcovers,-datagrid,-gridcontrol,-shaderuv}.js`.

## 0. The headline

**The ocean mask reads 255 — full water — on every term through the entire hole, and the field is
still absent.** Six further families were excluded by direct measurement. The whole hypothesis space
this arc was built on (coastline fidelity: NE re-assert, midcarve, texel density, carve, basemap
water) is retired at this coordinate, **which is the explanation for every null lever sweep**: they
were all mask levers, and the mask was never the problem.

## 1. The defect, measured

| | |
|---|---|
| hole | **8 CSS px = 16 device px = 836 m** at z9.3 (104.5 m/CSS px) |
| reproductions | **4 independent runs** — HOME camera, centred camera, ladder, and both grid-control legs |
| shape | a **hard step**, not a ramp: `\|ON−OFF\| = 0` for d = 0…7, then **94** at d = 8 |

⭐ **The step is what kills the "it is only a value gradient" reading — and that was MY alternative,
refuted by its own test.** `|ON−OFF| = 0` means the painted pixel is BYTE-IDENTICAL to its own
waves-OFF colour: the field is not faint there, it is *absent*. Comparing each pixel to itself
(rather than to a far-field reference) is the only comparison that can tell those apart, and it is
immune to the basemap restyle the Waves toggle causes.

## 2. Everything excluded, and by what

| candidate | measurement | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| a value gradient (nearshore ≠ offshore) | `\|ON−OFF\| = 0` through the hole | ❌ field is absent |
| **the mask data** | **base 255 · overlay 255 · effective 255 · `src: overlay_replace`, d = 0…7** | ❌ **mask is correct** |
| a covering basemap layer | identical layers inside the hole (`ocean-mask-inland-water`, `water-shadow`, `water`) and beyond it | ❌ nothing on top |
| overlay engagement | `coverage_gap` **and** `midcarve_replace` both give 836 m | ❌ |
| wave-data coverage | Florida grid **and** Madeira grid both give 836 m at fixed z9.3 | ❌ |
| basemap generalisation | basemap shoreline at the probe coord at every zoom (0/0/0/0/−6 m) | ❌ reference is sound |
| heightAlpha / blend-both | `u_heightAlphaEnabled` defaults 0 | ❌ |
| edge feather | keys on `grid_uv`: 0.428 from the edge vs a 0.18 width | ❌ |
| **shader mask-FETCH displacement** | `__GPU_DEBUG__.mode='mask'` — the shader's own mask edge is ~1 CSS px from the coast | ❌ samples correctly |

⚠️ The last row is the weakest: land/water luminance separated by only 169 → 188, barely over the
harness's own 12-unit gate. The *direction* (edge at the coast, not 8 px out) is safe; the "1 px" is not.

## 3. The zoom profile — the one property still unexplained

| zoom | m/CSS px | hole (CSS px) | **hole (m)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9.3 | 104.50 | 8 | **836** |
| 10.4 | 48.75 | 2 | 98 |
| 11.4 | 24.38 | 1 | 24 |
| 12.4 | 12.19 | 4 | 49 |
| 13.4 | 6.09 | 11 | 67 |

Fixed in neither pixels nor metres. **z9.3 is an 8.5× outlier**; from z10.4 up the coast is accurate
to 24–98 m, at or near the 2-CSS-px noise floor a clean bearing measures. So this is a **wide-zoom
phenomenon**, and the earlier "one bearing of 24 is broken" framing was really "one bearing at one
zoom".

⭐ **Reporting it in metres is what made this legible.** A fixed geographic error grows in pixels as
you zoom in and a fixed pixel error does not, so the unit choice decides whether a coarse-data
artifact looks like a shrinking one. Pixels alone would have hidden the outlier.

## 4. Where it must be, and the next experiment

Mask bytes right, shader fetch right, nothing covering, field absent ⇒ the suppression is **between
the mask sample and `gl_FragColor`** in `WebGLMarineShaders.js`.

**Leading hypothesis, NOT TESTED — do not treat it as a finding:** the bilinear tail of the
wave-height texture toward land cells. The regional grid is 8×8 over 1.93° ⇒ **~22.6 km per cell**,
so a fetch near the coast interpolates against a landward zero cell over kilometres, and
`waveBlend = smoothstep(0.0, 0.18, displayHeight)` (line ~562) drives the colour to nothing while
`oceanAlpha` stays 1. The shader's own comments already name this failure mode twice ("the bilinear
tail", "bilinear-to-zero edges cut the band short"). It also predicts the wide-zoom concentration,
because at z9.3 the coarse world data is in play where the regional tile is not.

**The experiment that would settle it:** instrument `displayHeight` the way the mask is already
instrumented — a `__GPU_DEBUG__` mode that paints the sampled height — then walk the same normal. If
height crosses 0.18 at d ≈ 8 while `oceanAlpha` is 1, it is proven; the fix is then a data-side
nearshore fill or a height floor over confirmed water, **not another mask change.**

## 5. Method notes worth keeping

- ⭐ **Two of my own hypotheses died here, both to their own controls** — "coverage_gap / coarse
  mask" and "wave-data region". The grid control printed **INCONCLUSIVE** rather than a verdict when
  a longer settle made both legs load the Madeira grid, and that refusal is what exposed the earlier
  Florida reading as a **settle artifact** rather than a cause. **A harness that reports its own
  precondition failure is worth more than one that always produces a number.**
- ⛔ `getStyle().layers` omits custom layers, so the covering-layer test read paint order from
  `map.style._order` (landmine re-applied from the 08-16 arc, not re-learned).
- ⭐ Camera choice decides WHICH MASK IS OPERATIVE: from the HOME camera the overlay is off and every
  `probeMaskGPU` read falls back to `coarse_base`, so **the first teardown never read the overlay
  mask at all**. Centring on the probe was required before the decisive 255 could be read.
