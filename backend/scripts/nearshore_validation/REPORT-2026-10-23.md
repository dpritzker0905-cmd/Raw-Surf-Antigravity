# Nearshore validation, first run: 2026-10-23

Offline only. No served number changed. Code at `dev` 9bf8d065c (worktree branch `claude/nearshore-validation`).
Raw numbers: `out/transfer.json`, `out/chain_eval.json` (local); summary fed to the reviewers: `results_summary.txt`.

## Why
The owner saw ~2-3 ft FACE at Cape/Port Canaveral on 2026-10-23 (~21Z) while the infobox read 6.5 ft. The surf chain
has no cape/shoal shadow model (exposure = cosine vs one shore normal, 0.595 floor, constant Kr 0.873).

## Data and controls
41009 offshore (51,255 records), CDIP 143 Cape nearshore (63,945), CDIP 134 Fort Pierce (55,600), 2023-01..2026-10.
Every spectrum rebuilt the same way (maximum entropy) and partitioned the same way (wavespectra `steepness`, default
cutoff, untuned). All controls pass (see README). Two real data bugs were caught by them: NDBC history r1/r2 x100,
and NDBC realtime `999.00` missing values.

## Finding 2: the chain's direction factor vs measured
- **Jetty Park (normal 110.7):** shape roughly right, but only because a jetty-local normal mimics the shadow; 0.09-0.17
  too generous for NE-ENE swell (chain 0.67/0.78 vs measured 0.58/0.65). Cocoa (normal 99.1) is worse: 0.73/0.82.
- **Open coast (N Hutchinson vs Fort Pierce):** the chain UNDER-reads oblique N/NNE swell (0.56/0.69 vs 0.86/0.82):
  refraction turns that swell toward shore (+16 deg measured) and a cosine rule cannot.
- Caveat (SubQ and Reflection both): buoy Hs at 10-16 m is not breaking height; this compares shapes and magnitudes of
  "how much reaches shore", not the breaking step.

## Finding 3: the breaking step (indicated, not validated)
Hold-out medians: chain from offshore input 3.8 ft vs from the nearshore buoy 3.0 ft at Jetty Park (Cocoa 3.9/3.1). The
one observation (2-3 ft face) against 4.8 ft (offshore input) and 5.3 ft (nearshore input). Feeding the already
refracted nearshore spectrum back through the chain double counts refraction. **n = 1: recorded, not a calibration.**
